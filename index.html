Residential

Your home should be a place to relax — not another item on your to-do list. At Martha's Cleaning Service, we take the hard work off your plate so busy families and professionals across the Twin Cities can come home to a space that's truly clean. Whether you need a one-time deep clean to reset your space, ongoing weekly or bi-weekly maintenance, or a thorough move-in/move-out cleaning, our team shows up prepared, on time, and ready to get to work.

We treat every home the way we'd want our own treated: with care, attention to detail, and respect for your belongings. From scrubbing baseboards and sanitizing kitchens to tackling bathrooms and high-traffic living areas, nothing gets overlooked. Flexible scheduling means we work around your life, not the other way around — and because we're a local, family-owned business, you'll always know who's walking through your door.

Commercial

A clean workplace makes a real difference — for your team's productivity, your clients' first impressions, and your business's reputation. Martha's Cleaning Service partners with small to mid-size offices throughout the Minneapolis/St. Paul metro to provide reliable, professional janitorial and office cleaning services. We handle everything from daily surface cleaning and restroom upkeep to trash removal, floor care, and common-area maintenance — all performed with the consistency your business depends on.

We understand that downtime costs money, which is why we build our schedules around yours. Early morning, evening, or weekend availability means your space gets cleaned without interrupting a single meeting or customer interaction. Our team is professionally trained, fully insured, and committed to delivering spotless results every visit. If your needs change, so do we — custom cleaning plans ensure you're only paying for exactly what you need.

About

Martha's Cleaning Service was built on a simple belief: everyone deserves a clean, comfortable space — and the people who provide that service deserve to be treated with dignity and respect. Founded five years ago right here in the Twin Cities, MCS is a proud local, family- and minority-owned business that started with a commitment to quality and has grown through the trust of our neighbors and community. What began as a small operation has become a team of dedicated cleaners who genuinely take pride in their work.

We're not a franchise or a faceless national chain — we're your neighbors, and this community is our community. That means we're invested in the long-term relationships we build with every client, and we hold ourselves accountable to a high standard each time we step through your door. When you choose MCS, you're supporting a local business that gives back to the metro area it calls home, while getting a level of personalized service that bigger companies simply can't match.
